Embed the 3D viewer

Placeholder page. The viewer stays inert until a visitor clicks, so it never captures page scrolling.

Copy-paste snippet

<div style="position:relative;width:100%;aspect-ratio:16/9">
  <iframe src="https://YOUR-HOST/viewer/index.html?embed=1&view=hero"
          title="Interactive 3D view of the project"
          allow="fullscreen" allowfullscreen loading="lazy"
          style="position:absolute;inset:0;width:100%;height:100%;border:0"></iframe>
</div>

Options

  • embed=1 shows the poster and a "View in 3D" button; the scene loads only after a click.
  • autostart=1 loads straight away (still inert until clicked). view= and tod= (0 to 1) set the start.
  • ui=min keeps only the view dock and the enquiry button.
  • postMessage v1 (inbound setView, setTime, tour) works only from origins listed in SPEC.embed.allowedOrigins. Outbound events: ready, viewChanged, ctaClicked, error.
